FROM THE OTHER SIDE OF THE BARRICADE

Riotkind didn't start with a market opportunity. It started with a concert ticket.

The road trips. The sweaty venues. The strangers who feel like friends by the second chorus. That moment when a band walks onstage and the whole room becomes one thing.

Founded in concept by a transgender woman and lifelong music lover, Riotkind is a promise to bring more of that feeling home to Kansas City. More shows. More opportunities for emerging artists. And eventually, a festival that belongs to the community as much as the crowd.

We're learning, planning, and building from the ground up. No corporate playbook. Just the conviction that the people who love live music should have a hand in its future.
