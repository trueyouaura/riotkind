/ 01 · THE STORY

WE WERE NEVER
HERE TO FIT IN.

Riotkind is an independent entertainment company in development, founded by a transgender woman in Kansas City, Missouri.

Our belief is simple: entertainment should bring people together, not tell them who gets to belong. We're starting with live music and festivals, and leaving room for everything else we might dream up.

We want to create unforgettable experiences, give emerging artists a platform, and build partnerships with people who believe dignity and respect aren't optional.

ALL THE NOISE.

01 /

02 /

ARTISTS ARE PEOPLE.

Fair treatment, clear communication, and respect for the people who make our events possible.

03 /

We intend to choose collaborators who uphold human rights and reject discrimination and harassment.

04 /

✳

OUR SIDE IS HUMANITY.

Riotkind stands against racism, transphobia, homophobia, antisemitism, Islamophobia, misogyny, ableism, xenophobia, and other forms of hatred. We support Palestinian freedom and self-determination, oppose Zionism as a political ideology, and reject the dehumanization or targeting of people based on their religion, ethnicity, nationality, or identity. We believe safety, freedom, and dignity belong to everyone.

Potential partners will be evaluated on documented conduct and commitments, never assumptions about personal identity.
